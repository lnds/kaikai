#   <target> <seconds>      cost of one light target, run alone
#   base <slice> <seconds>  fixed work of the shard that carries that slice
#                           (see the tier1-shard-N recipes in the Makefile)
#   default <seconds>       cost of a target not listed here
#   parallelism <n>         how many targets' worth of work a runner does
#                           at once under `make -j$(TEST_JOBS)`
#   contention <f>          fraction of the rest of its slice's cost the
#                           longest target absorbs while they share the CPU

# contention were fitted to the per-slice wall-clock of a green tier1 run,
# and the bases read from its shard logs. Re-measure when a shard drifts
